Learn How UTMs Work →

UTM parameters are labels added to a website link. They help analytics tools identify which email, social post, or advertising campaign brought someone to your website.

For example, label a newsletter link with the source “newsletter,” medium “email,” and campaign “spring_sale.” When visitors follow it, compatible analytics tools can report those campaign details.

Our UTM Builder puts these details into a campaign URL you can copy and share.

No. Start with your destination URL, Source, Medium, and Campaign Name. These tell your analytics where the link was shared, which channel it belongs to, and which campaign it supports.

Use the optional fields when you need more detail. Campaign ID identifies a campaign, Term labels a paid keyword, and Content distinguishes links such as a header button and a text link.

Keep your labels consistent. Using “newsletter” every time helps avoid splitting the same source into different entries in your reports.
